## Why

The full-width chart leads with a logarithmic scale rather than an answer about the next level, and a second level slider pushes the kill result below several controls on phones. A remembered level can connect the curve, a real-creature example, and the calculator without making the reader repeat the same input.

## What Changes

- Lead with experience to the next level and conditional kills of a published creature beside an earned-experience journey card.
- Keep one complete logarithmic chart always visible below the two cards, and every published breakpoint in a named disclosure.
- Use one visible remembered character-level control for the curve, calculator, and level-up talent example. Keep optional kill modifiers and calculation stages accessible without hiding the phone's immediate result.
- Explain assumptions in player language, keep the selected creature and place visible, and align controls with their results across screen widths.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `mechanics-pages`: Extend the Character Progression answer-first presentation while retaining its published curve, kill calculation, and other mechanics sections.

## Impact

Character Progression site components, level/kill calculation helpers and their tests; no publication schema or game rules change.
