## Why

A missing value showed as a dash with a hint. Beside a value it read as a stray mark, as in Gold's quantity "Unknown —", the same pattern that had made fixed creature levels read "Level 30 —".

## What Changes

- A missing value reads Unknown, muted and with its reason on hover, focus, and tap. Inside a sentence it reads as words such as "an unknown time".
- Gold without a valid amount reads Unknown in its quantity cell, with the reason "The game gives no valid gold amount for this drop".

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `compendium-reference`: how pages mark unknown values.

## Impact

Site only: the missing-value component and its uses in drops, sources, locations, availability, and requirements.
