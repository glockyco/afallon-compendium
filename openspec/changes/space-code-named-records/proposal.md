## Why

Some creature abilities and their effects carry the game's code names, such as BoarAttack1, BearAttack2, SkeletonAttack1, and HealingPotion. They titled pages and appeared in 92 links to Boar Attack 1 alone, where readers met them as unreadable identifiers.

## What Changes

- Ability and effect names that join capitalized words or end in a number read as separate words, and their slugs follow.
- Abbreviations such as AoE and DoT stay together, and items keep the names the game shows.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `entity-identity`: the names of code-named abilities and effects.

## Impact

Publication: record names and slugs of abilities and effects. The pages of these records move to spaced slugs.
