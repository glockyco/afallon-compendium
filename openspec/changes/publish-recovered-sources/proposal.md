## Why

The catalog records starting equipment, summoned creatures, object spawners, game-action grants, and loot-table membership that detail pages do not yet publish. Missing evidence makes obtainable entries look unavailable, while unrelated consumption actions and unbound loot tables must not be mistaken for ways to obtain them.

## What Changes

- Publish proven summon, spawner, starting inventory, and ability grant relationships on the corresponding detail pages.
- Identify loot-table membership without claiming an unbound table drops anywhere, and distinguish an item's self-consumption from an acquisition route.
- Render missing travel destinations without displaying raw scene identifiers or suggesting a scene exists in this game version.
- Add boundary tests and verify representative published pages.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `recovered-source-evidence`: Reader-visible relationships backed by catalog entities, game actions, and source bindings.

### Modified Capabilities
- None. Existing detail-page and publication requirements remain unchanged; this adds missing evidence without relaxing those requirements.
