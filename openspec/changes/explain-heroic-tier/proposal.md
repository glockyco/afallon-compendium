## Why

The Heroic Tier page never said how to turn the tier on or off, where the consoles stand, what an empowered creature or an affix does, or that Bosses and World Quests pay more currency. It ended with a raw list of settings, such as a gear scaling coefficient of 0.0008, that a reader could not use.

## What Changes

- New verified rules from native evidence: entering and leaving at a Heroic Console, the three console places, the recommended level, the saved character choice, the five places and dungeon states where the tier pauses, empowered creature health and damage with the gear-score bonus and its cap, affix chances and each affix, the affix loot multiplier, Boss and World Quest currency, the Essence base, and the Heroic Ascension trees. Three unknown rules that these answer leave the record.
- The page opens with the overview and three short answers with links: turn it on, what changes, and what you get. Its sections follow in three parts: Getting started, What changes, and Rewards. A table shows creature strength at three gear scores, and the Essence table names each rank's multiplier.
- The raw settings section leaves the page, because every value now reads in a rule sentence or a table. The World Quests page links Heroic currency instead.
- The opening and part layout of the Crafting and Gathering page becomes two shared components that both pages use.
