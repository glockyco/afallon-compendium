## Why

The Adventurers guide placed Dungeon Finder parties last, after jobs and gear, although it and Meeting and inviting are both about getting adventurers into your party. A reader who wants a party had to skip over the two sections about what adventurers do on their own.

## What Changes

- The Adventurers guide orders its sections Meeting and inviting, Dungeon Finder parties, Jobs and progress, and Gear upgrades.
