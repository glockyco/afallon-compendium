## Why

Every column of the Browse menu has a place on the home page except Mechanics: the six guides were reachable only through the menu, although they explain the numbers on every other page.

## What Changes

- The home page gets a Mechanics section after Crafting and gathering with a card for each guide, naming it with the published sentence that says what it explains.
- The Browse menu and the home page read the guides from one list, so they stay in the same order.
