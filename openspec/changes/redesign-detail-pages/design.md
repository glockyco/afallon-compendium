## Context

See proposal.md for motivation. The site dispatches published documents through `DetailPage.svelte` to kind-specific Svelte components in `apps/site/src/lib/detail/pages/`. Shared `Hero`, `Section`, `RelationTable`, tooltip, navigation, and CSS modules currently implement the single-column layout. Publication and contracts live in `packages/publication` and `packages/contracts`. The approved brief is `local/design/BRIEF.md`; the HTML/CSS and 1440/390 screenshots in `local/design/mockups/` illustrate item, creature, node, and hover surfaces. Pink `.note` copy in those mockups is an annotation, not UI.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** Keep one clear acquisition/action answer above relations for every kind; preserve linked game facts and existing deep links while reducing visual duplication. Make compact layouts readable without clipping at desktop and phone widths. Retain the skill and character level curves and linked guide explanations.

**Non-Goals:** Change game rules, invent unavailable rewards or placement probabilities, redefine the game map, or adopt a general-purpose page builder. No fixed default for missing authored facts.

## Decisions

1. **Shared frame, kind-specific answers.** Introduce a title/answer/aside/rest composition reused by the kind-specific pages. At widths of at least 1024 px, reserve a 20rem side column starting at title level and keep it visible while scrolling; below that width, order the title, answer, side facts, and relations vertically. The side content is not a second hero. A kind with no applicable strip omits it; one strip holds at most five decisive facts. Shared tokens carry the existing warm dark palette, rarity colors, serif titles, and sans-serif body across detail, list, hub, and guide surfaces. Use `html { font-size: 100% }`, `rem` type sizes, a 1rem body with 1.5 line height, and a .875rem floor. No tracked uppercase labels; test each text/surface combination at 4.5:1 and interactive targets at 24 by 24 px or larger. Do not make color the only signifier.

2. **Preview rows and canonical full relations.** Present up to eight compact rows per section with an icon or portrait, linked name, level/place subline, count and one comparable value. Keep the full relation and existing row anchors behind Show N more; opening an anchored hidden row reveals it before scrolling. Preserve distinct condition/chance/quantity rows and exact counterpart coverage. Group node and creature locations by published place, count distinct map spots, sort by count, and send an explicit map selection to the map for individual spots; never enumerate hundreds of numbered links. Retain conditions, variants, and unknown/unplaced entries on expansion. Place sections use category counts; boss rows use portraits.

3. **Answers and side facts follow the approved page plans.** Implement each kind's order in the brief. Item source routes are grouped by kind and ordered by guaranteed yield then known spot count; conditional probabilities stay labeled and must not be presented as guarantees. Recipes use an equation with ingredient quantities, skill gate, station, and a linked teacher when known. The product page shows only materials in its recipe equation so its game tooltip is not duplicated. Quest objectives become a numbered checklist; the quest chain moves to a vertical side stepper. Node yield bonus and crafting experience breakpoints remain computed sentences. Skills retain the level curve chart and level control in the aside, with recipes grouped into required-level bands. Class talent trees retain the established tab/anchor behavior. Secondary data occupies the final Details block or closed disclosure, not an inaccessible deletion.

4. **Rules have one explanatory home.** Preserve reviewed rule provenance and conditional placement data in the catalog, but resolve entity placements to guide-step links and computed facts, never copy rule prose into entity sections, label hints, or hover cards. Mechanics guides lead with an overview and ordered steps, then a worked example and key values, with the full evidence-backed rule list closed. Guide links target the pertinent step rather than the closed rule list. When inputs or a calculation are unverified, omit the computed number and state the limit; do not infer probabilities from raw weights.

5. **Hover previews are distinct from page content.** Keep the existing right-start/left-start positioning and vertical containment, plus fixed bottom treatment on narrow screens. Render only the game tooltip and one context line for items/abilities, or portrait/icon, level, place, and one context line for other kinds. No bands, multirow tables, or rule prose. Keyboard focus and tap open the same facts; pointer movement upward or across neighboring entries must not be intercepted by the old card. Every preview fact remains reachable on a page.

6. **Publication computes facts, UI presents them.** Derive location spot counts per place and aggregate for the item/node/NPC routes from published placements, respecting identity and deduplicating a spot after equivalent-row merges. Derive spawner option shares at the lowest and highest supported skill levels by normalizing verified eligible weights in each source spawner, then aggregate only when the interpretation across spawners is meaningful and labeled; do not imply an unverified attunement bonus. Include creature portrait references for relation rows. Investigate `For Sale 2500 Gold` and `SkeletonAttack1 NPC` against authored identity and verified relations: use a truthful reader label if supported, otherwise record unresolved data and avoid fabricated names. The inverted Barrowdeep Deathguard gold bounds (15–3) must be repaired at the verified source or reported explicitly as unresolved; never silently swap or display an impossible range.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Collapsing relations can conceal an anchored target: expansion, tab selection, and scroll must cooperate with fragments and browser history.
- A source kind can have no guaranteed yield or known spots: sorting must keep the distinction between known and unknown, and explain the route without claiming certainty.
- Spawn shares across spawners with different weights are not automatically a single probability: publish separately or state the precise aggregate denominator before displaying one percentage.
- Sticky side content may exceed the viewport: cap its scrollable area without hiding keyboard focus or introducing horizontal overflow.
- The mockups cover only four surfaces; other kinds follow the approved brief and existing published data rather than copying placeholder examples.
