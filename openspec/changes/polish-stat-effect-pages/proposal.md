## Why

Stat and effect pages lead with default values and implementation labels while useful sources and outcomes are scattered into unstructured lists. Some effect records offer no player-facing outcome. Item filters also omit percent variants of the same stat.

## What Changes

- Present stat sources with amounts and variants in shared sortable tables, and offer a single item link covering all variants.
- Put effect outcomes first, compare changing ranks only, merge repeated applications, and omit standalone effects with no usable outcome.
- Sort stats by occurrence, distinguish on-hit triggers, and describe effect browsing beyond buffs and debuffs.
- Avoid unverified damage-family assertions when source fields disagree.

## Impact

Changes the stat document schema and the projected stat and effect publication. Rebuild the candidate before accepting it.
