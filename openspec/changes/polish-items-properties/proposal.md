# Polish item and property reference pages

## Why

Enchanting items bury their effect beneath an unframed secondary list. Crafted routes repeat skill requirements and expose a level control before the recipe is clear. Equipment controls sit loose under the tooltip. Properties duplicate their three prices, and some item pages cannot answer either how to get or how to use the item.

## What changes

- Show enchanting effects and equipment options in shared side cards, keeping the crafted route readable and its level-by-level experience available on demand.
- Lead unsourced but useful items with their actual use instead of framing two missing answers. Suppress tautological self-consumption and empty usage sections.
- Withhold three reviewed item records with no identified source or effect, while making the evidence check fail if a new fact or inbound relation makes them useful.
- Present property purchase and income facts once in the shared side-card system, and link an exact matching sign area when that place has a page.
- Expose the linked effects applied by consumables, item abilities, and on-hit stats, including verified duration and conditional chance.

## Impact

Item and property detail components, publication projections, item effect document contracts, shared exclusion evidence, and the item/property presentation specification.
