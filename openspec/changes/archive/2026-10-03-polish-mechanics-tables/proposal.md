# Polish mechanics tables and navigation

## Why

Long mechanics reference tables currently use unrelated layouts and often expose every row. Combat and Loot bury important answers in long prose, World Quests lacks a direct route to matching quests, and featured links and catalog relationships miss the site's entity previews.

## What changes

- Present entity comparisons through the shared sortable relation table and numeric calculations through the shared reference table style. Limit long inline tables to eight initial rows with a reveal control.
- Break dense mechanics explanations into useful subsections and linked examples. Preserve level-curve and skill experience references.
- Link World Quests to its filtered quest catalog, use entity previews for featured and relation-valued links, and keep coverage gaps readable behind disclosure.

## Impact

The site mechanics pages, hub, catalog relation cells, coverage route and presentation components change. Published list relation references require a versioned list-document schema and a republished candidate.
