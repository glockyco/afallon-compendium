# Link Heroic Affixes To Their Effects

## Why

The Heroic Tier page names its affixes without their recognizable in-game icons. Their effect records have no pages because the tier runtime, rather than an ability or item, applies them. A reader cannot move between the combat explanation and an effect's details.

## What Changes

- Link verified Heroic rules to the named status effects, including the tier buff and the two secondary effects.
- Treat a verified mechanics rule's effect link as a publication reachability edge. Preserve reviewed exclusions when an effect is content-free.
- Show the verified explanation and a link to its mechanics section on each reachable effect page.

## Impact

The catalog rules record, effect publication and schema, and effect detail presentation change. No map or console implementation changes.
