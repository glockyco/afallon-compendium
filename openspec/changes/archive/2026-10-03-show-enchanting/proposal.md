## Why

Enchanting items currently show only an unlinked enchantment name, leaving players unable to see what gear fits, what the item adds, or how to apply it.

## What Changes

- Publish each enchanting item's captured gear requirements, tier bonuses, success rate, time, and listed additional costs on its item page.
- Link enchantment references and search aliases to that item's Enchants section, including pairs with different names.
- Explain verified application rules and list all enchanting items and known sources in Crafting and Gathering.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `enchanting`: Player-facing item details, links, search, and guide information about enchanting.
