## Why

List and gallery views currently disagree on their controls and omit or repeat player-facing facts. Short pages and map-capable search hits have related presentation gaps visible in the same review.

## What Changes

- Share gallery and table search and result-count controls, with matching position and sentence-case text.
- Show property prices with available currency and payment intervals, including graceful fallbacks and wide scene artwork.
- Keep browse cards and tables concise, informative, readable, and accessible across desktop and phone widths.
- Link placed gathering-node search results to the map and seat the footer at the bottom of short pages.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `list-filters`: Present useful browse facts without duplicate or misleading rows and preserve readable list controls.
- `reference-layout`: Keep map actions available on placed node searches and the shared footer at the bottom of a short page.

## Impact

Site browse components, static list/search projections and kind registry, and the shared page shell. The static publication candidate must be regenerated to carry changed projections.
