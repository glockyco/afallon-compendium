## Why

Site search results stayed open after the reader left the field or followed a result, covering the next page's heading and actions. The results also had no keyboard path beyond tabbing through links.

## What Changes

- Results close when focus leaves the search, on Escape, and after any navigation, which also clears the field.
- Clicking the field, typing, or an arrow key reopens the current query's results.
- Arrow keys move a visible highlight through the results, and Enter opens the highlighted page.

## Impact

Site: the shared search component used in the header and on the home page.
