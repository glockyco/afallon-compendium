## Why

The Character Progression page put the kill calculator second, before the level curve. Most readers come for the experience that each level needs and for the rules, not for the calculator. Several sections also read poorly:
- The talent point section repeated its heading and called its limit "the recorded maximum".
- The level curve section explained the chart twice.
- The experience sources counted creatures by their record level, although most of them spawn near the character's level within a zone range.

## What Changes

- Order the page by reader need: overview, level curve, steps, talent points, experience sources, level difference, the calculator, and the rules reference. The owner chose the level curve before the steps.
- Make the overview three sentences that lead into the level curve.
- Explain the level curve once.
- State talent points in one paragraph: the start amount, the gain per level-up, the total at the level cap, and the limit. Show no extra heading when there is one point type.
- Count creatures with experience by how they spawn, at a fixed level or near the character's level within a zone range. Use the published spawn levels that the NPC pages show. Show the source facts as a list.
- Name the level difference columns by the creature's position relative to the character. Add a row for creatures without a level modifier.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `mechanics-pages`: Character Progression orders its sections by reader need, counts experience sources by spawn level, and states talent points plainly.

## Impact

- Publication: the Character Progression overview text and the experience source counts. The mechanics schema does not change.
- Site: the Character Progression page.
- Archive after `publish-corrupted-gear`, whose version of "Mechanics pages are guides" this change extends, and after `add-kill-calculator`, which adds the calculator section.
