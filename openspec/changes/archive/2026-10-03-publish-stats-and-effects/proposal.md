## Why

The site names stats and effects across items, abilities, talents, and requirements, but readers cannot open those references to learn what they do or where they come from. Verified combat rules and recorded recovery values also need a player-facing home.

## What Changes

- Publish player-relevant stats and source-connected effects as linked entity pages, lists, search results, and tooltips.
- Explain each stat's recorded definition, on-hit effects, recovery, and linked sources, and each effect's rank outcomes, timing, sources, and requirements without asserting unverified behavior.
- Add a Combat guide with registered native evidence for stat composition, gear sets, damage and defense, critical hits, on-hit triggers, and lasting effects.
- Add Stats and Effects to Character navigation and Combat to guide navigation. Introduce static stat and effect schemas v1 and mechanics v17.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `stats-and-effects`: Selection, publication, source links, and display of stats and effects and the evidence-bounded Combat guide.

### Modified Capabilities
- None. The new capability adds requirements without replacing existing requirements.

## Impact

Contracts, publication projections and catalog reference selection, published resources and lists, site detail pages, navigation, and the registered mechanics-rules record change. Existing gear set, currency, station, race, and faction page shapes do not change.
