## Why

The NPC and quest lists opened every filter group. Place lists 65 places, quest Area and Chain list 37 areas and 31 chains, and Faction sits between them, so the short groups that most readers use were pushed far down the panel.

## What Changes

- On the NPC list, Place and Faction start closed. On the quest list, Area and Chain start closed. The other groups of both lists start open.
- A group with an active filter still opens, so a shared link shows its own filters.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `list-filters`: the groups that start open on the NPC and quest lists.

## Impact

Site only: the default open groups of the list filter panel.
