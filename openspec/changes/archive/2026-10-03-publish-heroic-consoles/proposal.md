## Why

The accepted scans locate three Heroic Consoles, but their typed source records have no map marker. Readers cannot open the exact console location from the Heroic Tier page or the places where they stand.

## What Changes

- Project the already retained, position-bearing Heroic Console source details into a distinct default-visible map marker category and a contextual selected-marker card.
- Associate each published console placement with its named place using the observed hierarchy and publish those associations in the Heroic Tier and place documents.
- Link each console spot directly to map selection from both its mechanics page and its place page.

## Impact

- Affects marker contracts, map publication, public mechanics/place documents, the map preview, and the related detail pages.
- Uses existing catalog placements with their scanned scene, coordinates, map binding, area, and evidence. No changes to unrelated mechanics rules or effect publication.
