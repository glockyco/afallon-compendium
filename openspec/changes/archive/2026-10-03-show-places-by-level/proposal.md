## Why

The hub grouped places into level bands with plain names. The recorded ranges are scaling ranges and eight places share levels 1 to 20, so the bands hardly separated anything, the cards had uneven heights, and nothing said what a place holds. Every place has artwork and records its creatures and quests.

## What Changes

- "Places by level" shows each place outside the dungeons as a tile with its artwork, its level range, and its counts of creatures and quests, in range order, with places that record no creature and no quest last. Eight tiles show first, then Show N more.
- A "Your level" field beside the heading saves the reader's character level, the same level the mechanics calculators use. The tiles follow each keystroke. The places whose range contains the level come first and carry a "Your level" mark, and an empty field forgets the level.

### Modified Capabilities

- `compendium-hub`: the place entry by level.
