## Why

The Heroic Tier guide gives a gear bonus setting without showing what equipment can actually gain the Heroic state or what that state does to the item's displayed stats.

## What Changes

- Verify the creature-loot origin and Heroic stat application against build-matched native code.
- Explain the verified Heroic gear rules in their own Heroic Tier guide section.
- Preview a Heroic variant of gear with a recorded eligible creature drop on the item page, without implying that crafted gear or quest rewards can become Heroic.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `heroic-gear`: Verified Heroic loot eligibility, rules, and item-level stat preview.
