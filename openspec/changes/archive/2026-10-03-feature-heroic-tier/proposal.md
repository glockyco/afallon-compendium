# Feature Heroic Tier Among the Mechanics

## Why

Heroic Tier is one of the mechanics a reader asks about first, but the Browse panel and the home page only reach it through the list of all mechanics. Factions and Reputation took that place, although readers look it up less often.

## What Changes

- The Mechanics column of the Browse panel and the Mechanics section of the home page name Heroic Tier instead of Factions and Reputation. Both lists stay in alphabetical order.
- Factions and Reputation stays reachable from the list of all mechanics pages.

## Impact

The featured flags of the mechanics topic definitions change. No publication data changes.
