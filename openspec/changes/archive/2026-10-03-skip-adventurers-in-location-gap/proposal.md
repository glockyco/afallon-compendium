# Skip Adventurers in the Location Gap

## Why

The coverage page listed 180 NPCs without a map location, and 115 of them were adventurers. Adventurers roam the world and join a party by invitation, so no spawner places them and their missing location is not a gap.

## What Changes

- The `npcWithoutLocation` gap leaves out adventurers, so the list names only the NPCs whose location is truly unknown.

## Impact

Publication coverage changes. No page or schema changes.
