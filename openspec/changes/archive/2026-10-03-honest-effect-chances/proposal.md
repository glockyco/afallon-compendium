## Why

Several percentages on stat, ability, effect, and item pages currently omit the event that rolls them. This can make conditional effects look like flat chances per hit or cast, and a zero sentinel for an always-applied item action can appear as a zero percent chance.

## What Changes

- Explain the stat trigger roll separately from each on-hit effect roll on stat pages, tooltips, and item pages.
- Identify applied-effect percentages as chances per eligible application attempt rather than per cast or hit.
- State direct item-effect chance per use, omit the always-applied zero sentinel, and explain random-stat selection rolls.
- Explain that an item limit can discard successful chest-content rolls.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `stats-and-effects`: Clarify the two on-hit chance gates and application attempts on stat and effect surfaces.
- `item-property-presentation`: Clarify direct-use effects, random-stat chances, and capped chest contents.

## Impact

Site stat, ability, NPC, effect, and item presentation, plus item-effect publication. No changes to creature drops, world loot, gathering, enchanting, or random-spot probabilities.
