## Why

The Variants table on a creature page put every differing fact of a version into one wide row. Stats, abilities, and long place labels crowded the cells, ability lists wrapped into columns of single names, and six versions ran off the side of the table. Readers could not compare two versions at a glance.

## What Changes

- The Variants section compares the versions side by side: one column per version, headed by its label and its portrait when the portraits differ, and one row per fact in which the versions differ, with each stat as its own row.
- The versions share the width of the section evenly and center their values. When they do not fit, they wrap into blocks whose sizes differ by at most one, each block repeating the fact names, so the comparison never scrolls sideways. A phone shows one version per block as a list of facts.
- A block leaves out a fact that none of its versions has. When every listed level scales with the player, the section line says so once instead of in every cell.
