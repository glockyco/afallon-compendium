## Why

Ability, class, skill, and gathering node pages vary sharply in usefulness. Timer-only abilities have no player answer, several tables repeat invariant values, and a fit-to-screen talent web makes links too small to use by touch.

## What Changes

- Withhold abilities that have neither an effect nor a known learner or user, while leaving references readable.
- Lead effectful abilities without known users with their effect instead of an empty users card.
- Focus a readable talent-web arm, add a tree navigator, and keep pan, zoom, and List.
- Move small groups of class, skill, and gathering facts into shared side cards, condense recipe and gear tables, and offer secondary progression behind a disclosure.
- Format long node timers in human units and distinguish needed versus consumed items in node tooltips.

## Impact

Ability publication inclusion and detail pages for abilities, classes, skills, and gathering nodes change. The published node timers and skill level curves do not change.
