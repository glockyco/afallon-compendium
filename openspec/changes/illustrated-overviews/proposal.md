## Why

Small catalog overviews reduce illustrated choices to table rows and Skills interleave unrelated activities. Readers need to recognize and compare choices without losing the existing detailed table.

## What Changes

- Share one tile visual system between the home page and six small overviews.
- Present Classes, Skills grouped by Crafting, Gathering, and Weapon, Races, Factions, Properties, and art-free Mechanics as linked editorial tiles with published, context-specific facts.
- Keep the sortable table available, especially for comparing property prices and income.
- Publish class descriptions, skill starting status, faction starting standings, and property currencies and income intervals in list rows, where their detail pages already expose those facts.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `illustrated-overviews`: Small catalog gallery and comparison behavior across devices.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The existing table behavior remains available.
